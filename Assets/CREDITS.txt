CREDITS - Mini Project 1: The Enchanted Forest Shrine
CSE-411 Introduction to Game Development
=====================================================================

All third-party assets below were imported as individual assets only.
No demo scene from any pack was used. The terrain, layout, shrine,
ruins, lighting and scripts were built by me for this project.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
CREATED / MODIFIED BY ME
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Terrain sculpting, terrain layer setup and texture painting
- Scene layout: forest entrance path, ancient ruins, main shrine clearing
- Shrine and ruins arrangement, rune circle, light beam
- Crystal mesh (Assets/Models/Crystal.asset) made by me for the glowing crystals
- Terrain tree prefabs (Assets/Prefabs/TerrainTrees) with upright baked meshes
  (Assets/Models/TerrainTrees) made from the Quaternius models for the Terrain tree tool
- Custom / modified materials in Assets/Materials (Shrine, Terrain, Foliage, Grass)
  including emissive crystal and rune materials, ForestSky skybox material
- Lighting setup: green-tinted directional light, fog, point/spot shrine lights
- C# scripts in Assets/Scripts:
  ExplorerController, CameraViewpointSwitcher, FloatingCrystal, LightFlicker
